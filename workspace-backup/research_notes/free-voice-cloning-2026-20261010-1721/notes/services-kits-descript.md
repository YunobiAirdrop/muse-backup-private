### Facts
- Free: $0 — 15 conversion minutes, 0 voice slots, 0 download minutes. Official FAQ: "Professional and Instant voice cloning is unavailable on this tier." No downloads on free.
- Starter $10/mo: unlimited conversions, 2 voice slots, 15 download minutes, Instant Cloning. Producer $30/mo: unlimited slots, 60 download min, instant + professional cloning. Professional $60/mo: unlimited downloads (fair use).
- Built for SINGING/voice conversion (vocals, harmonies, stems) — not speech TTS. Poor fit for voiceover use case.
- Billing is by download minutes; custom voices freeze if you cancel (scored-tools).

### Facts
- Free: $0 — limited AI tools, 60 media minutes/month (ssbcrack Oct 2026). Overdub on free: trial only — stock voice (pilottools) / 1,000-word vocabulary trial (pexo).
- Custom Overdub voice clone requires Hobbyist: $16/mo billed annually or $24 monthly (ssbcrack Oct 2026); older sources cite $12/mo annual.
- Hard constraints: clone YOUR OWN voice only (strict consent; collaborator authorization possible); training needs ~10-30 min of clear English speech; 24-48h processing (qcall).
- Quality: short fixes hard to distinguish; longer passages flatten vs ElevenLabs/Typecast (toolnest).
- Verdict for mission: unsuitable — needs 10-30 min ENGLISH training audio, own-voice only, and paid tier for custom clone.
