## Policy background (established from multiple sources)
- On July 15, 2025, YouTube renamed its "repetitious content" YPP policy to "inauthentic content" and clarified it covers repetitive/mass-produced content. YouTube called it a "minor update"/clarification, not a new ban. Reused content policy unchanged.
- Key July 2025 target examples: template videos with tiny changes; slideshow videos with identical voiceover or none; AI-generated videos with zero creative input; copied content across a channel.
- Feb 2026 update (moneywise/TechCrunch): three named categories of ineligible content: (1) repetitive/generic/template-based, (2) "off-putting" emotionally manipulative content, (3) AI personas on sensitive topics (finance, legal, health, politics).
- July 2026 update per techtimes: "unsatisfying or off-putting content" extended to content that "relies heavily on emotionally manipulative formulas" and "mimics existing formats or stories to a degree that the videos feel interchangeable" even when technically distinct.
