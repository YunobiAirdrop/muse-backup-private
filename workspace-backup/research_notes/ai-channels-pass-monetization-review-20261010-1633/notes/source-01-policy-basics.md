Key facts:
1. July 15, 2025: "repetitious content" renamed to "inauthentic content"; clarified to cover repetitive/mass-produced content. YouTube called it a minor clarification, not a new ban. Reused content policy unchanged.
2. Target examples: template videos with tiny changes; slideshow videos with identical voiceover or none; AI-generated videos with zero creative input; copied content across a channel; narrated stories with only superficial differences; image slideshows with little/no narrative, commentary, or educational value; AI content made from generic templates without the creator's own insight.
3. AI use itself is NOT disqualifying. TeamYouTube: "We welcome creators using AI tools to enhance their storytelling, and channels that use AI in their content remain eligible to monetize."
4. Review looks at the CHANNEL as a whole: main theme, most viewed videos, newest videos, biggest proportion of watch time. Decision typically takes ~1 month.
5. Allowed: same intro/outro on every video if main content changes; series with recurring characters if each episode has its own story.
6. July 16 (2026, per moneywise citing TechCrunch): three named categories ineligible — (a) repetitive/generic/template-based incl. unoriginal tutorials; (b) "off-putting" emotionally manipulative content (e.g., animal-peril loops); (c) AI personas on sensitive topics (finance, legal, health, politics).
7. July 2026 per techtimes: "unsatisfying or off-putting" extended to content "mimics existing formats or stories to a degree that the videos feel interchangeable" even when technically distinct — aggregate feel matters, not just literal duplication. Also: AI Personas Related to Sensitive Topics (AI doctor, AI financial advice, AI legal interpretation) barred from monetization. In Jan 2026 YouTube wiped channels under "generic or repetitive content" category (~35M subscribers affected per headline).
8. Disclosure: altered/synthetic realistic content must be disclosed via YouTube Studio; disclosure does NOT reduce reach or monetization eligibility. Not disclosing when required can lead to removal/suspension from YPP. AI script/thumbnail/caption/audio-cleanup assistance does not require disclosure. Cloning your own voice for voiceover does not need disclosure per multiple sources (kveeky, akool).
