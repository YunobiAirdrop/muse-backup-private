## Bought TikTok affiliate accounts inherit the seller's record
A listing labeled “1,000 followers, affiliate active” sells you only the follower count, since [TikTok Shop's 2026 affiliate rules](https://seremonia.id/industri/teknologi/beli-akun-tiktok-affiliate-amankah-ini-aturan-tiktok-shop-2026/) tie eligibility to the person behind the account: 18 or older, with verified KTP, bank details, and a clean creator record. The account's hidden history comes along too, and under the CHR rating system past violations can freeze commissions for up to 90 days while under review.
