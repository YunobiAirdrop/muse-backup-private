## Description
New English-language YouTube channel dedicated to strange and unique stories, created at Yunobi's request and set to Shorts-only. It follows the short documentary style of the silkhenge reference reel: three different stories go out daily from a 32-story bank with no repeats, using curiosity hooks, licensed stock footage, and English voiceover, airing at US peak hours (21:00, 03:00, 07:00 WIB).
