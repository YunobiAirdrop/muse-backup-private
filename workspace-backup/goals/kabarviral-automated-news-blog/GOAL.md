# KabarViral automated news blog

Goal ID: goal_9cabbb0b7fdb
Goal slug: kabarviral-automated-news-blog

## Description
An automated mixed-topic news blog on Blogger that fetches news from RSS, rewrites articles with AI, applies SEO, and auto-posts daily to grow traffic and earn revenue. The pipeline and daily 07:11 cron are live at kabarviralterkiniterbaru.blogspot.com with 4 static pages and navigation published. The custom theme is being debugged: restores install fine but the homepage renders no posts, and a v1.11 minimal diagnostic theme has been issued to isolate the cause.
