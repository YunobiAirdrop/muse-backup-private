October 1962. For thirteen days, the world stood on the edge of nuclear war, and most people had no idea how close we came.

I have always been fascinated by moments when history hangs by a thread, and the Cuban Missile Crisis is the most dramatic of them all. In this video, we start on Black Saturday, October 27th, the day a U-2 spy plane was shot down over Cuba and Soviet ships steamed toward an American blockade. Then we rewind to see how it all began: the secret missiles, the tense White House meetings, the submarine officer who said no, and the secret deal that saved the world.
