She was the grandest ship ever built — and the world swore she could never sink. This is the true story of the night arrogance met ice in the North Atlantic.

On April 14, 1912, the RMS Titanic struck an iceberg at 11:40 PM. Warnings ignored, a rescue ship asleep nineteen kilometers away, a band that played until the very end — and fifteen hundred souls lost in freezing water. This documentary follows the Titanic from her proud departure in Southampton to her final plunge at 2:20 AM, and the legacy that changed maritime law forever.
