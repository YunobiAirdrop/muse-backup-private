The numbers tell one last story. OECD data shows annual hours falling in countries like Germany and Denmark — under fourteen hundred a year — while Americans grind near eighteen hundred. Same technology, wildly different choices. Policies, unions, culture — THOSE decide your hours far more than any machine ever will. Medieval workers had the church calendar protecting their rest. Modern workers have... a calendar app full of meetings. The six-hundred-year lesson is this: free time is never handed down by progress. It is taken — by rules, by culture, by people deciding that enough is enough. Think about that for a second. Progress did not shorten the workweek. People did.
