And here is the modern trap. Those sixteen-hundred-and-twenty-hour medieval workers had no retirement accounts, no health insurance, no weekends at the mall. But they also had no burnout, no hustle culture, no four a.m. email checks. Modern productivity made us richer — and more exhausted. Study after study finds that past about fifty hours a week, human output collapses — we are just warming chairs and calling it dedication. So maybe the real question is not "how do we work less, like peasants?" It is: why did all our brilliant machines fail to set us free? The answer is simpler than you think — and it has nothing to do with technology.
