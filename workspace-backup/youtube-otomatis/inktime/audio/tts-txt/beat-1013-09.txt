So why should YOU care about something that happened three thousand years ago? Because our world looks EXACTLY like the Late Bronze Age. Think about it: our entire civilization runs on global supply chains. Computer chips from one island. Rare earth metals from a handful of mines. One broken link — a pandemic, a war, a drought — and whole industries stop. Cline himself has said the parallels are uncomfortable. What fascinates me about this is the real lesson: the Bronze Age did not fall to one great enemy. It fell because it was TOO connected, TOO specialized, TOO dependent on a system nobody could control. Complexity was its superpower — and its fatal flaw. The palaces did not see it coming. The question is: would we?
