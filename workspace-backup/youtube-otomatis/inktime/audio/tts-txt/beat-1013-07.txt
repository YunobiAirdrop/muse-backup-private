Here is what "collapse" actually looked like, and it is worse than you imagine. This was not a slow decline. Archaeologists find a layer of ash and burned rubble — a destruction layer — at site after site, all from the same generation. The Hittite capital, Hattusa, was burned and abandoned — never rebuilt. The Mycenaean palaces of Greece — Pylos, Mycenae, Tiryns — burned with their storerooms full. And then something chilling: the Greeks forgot how to write. Their script, Linear B, simply vanished. For four hundred years, Greece produced almost no written records at all.  Think about that for a second. An entire civilization that FORGOT HOW TO READ AND WRITE. The palaces were gone. The scribes were gone. The knowledge was gone. That is not a recession. That is a civilizational hard reset.
