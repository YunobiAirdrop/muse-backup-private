Okay — here's what I think, honestly. The real difference is not the number of hours. It is who CONTROLS them. A medieval peasant could not choose to work more even if he wanted to. The sun set. The church bell rang. The feast day arrived — and work simply stopped. There was no email at midnight. No boss texting on a Sunday. Compare that to us: the modern worker is connected to work twenty-four seven. The factory whistle never blows anymore... because it lives in our pocket. We traded the tyranny of the harvest for the tyranny of the inbox. And I am honestly not sure we got the better deal. What fascinates me about this is that we invented every labor-saving machine in history... and somehow ended up working MORE than people with wooden plows.
