Historians now call the decades after five thirty-six the Late Antique Little Ice Age — a prolonged cooling that lasted over a century. A twenty sixteen study in Nature Geoscience showed that the eruptions of five thirty-six and five forty triggered the coldest decade in the last two thousand years, and the chill lingered for more than a hundred years. Empires weakened. The great Justinian Plague burned through populations. Trade shrank. And in Scandinavia, archaeologists link this cooling to the mysterious abandonment of villages — and possibly even to the origin of the Norse myth of Ragnarok, the fabled winter that never ends. A volcanic winter so terrible it became mythology.
