Just when it seemed the worst was over — it got worse. The ice cores show a second enormous sulfate spike, dated to around five forty. Another eruption. Possibly another supervolcano. The dust veil thickened again. Ancient writers describe the sun staying dim for years, not months. Procopius wrote that the eclipse of the sun's light lasted the whole year — and frankly, the sky stayed strange long after. Modern climate reconstructions suggest global temperatures plunged by two to three degrees Celsius. That sounds small. It is not. Two degrees was the difference between harvest and famine.
