Now close your eyes and imagine standing on the surface of a rogue planet. The ground beneath you is frozen solid, harder than steel. Above you, the sky is pure black, but it is not empty. Thousands of stars burn with a clarity you have never seen on Earth, because there is no atmosphere glow, no sunlight washing them out. And there, dominating the sky, is the faint band of the Milky Way, the galaxy this world is drifting through, alone, forever.
