Then came a discovery nobody expected. In twenty twenty three, the James Webb Space Telescope looked deep into the Orion Nebula and found dozens of Jupiter sized objects, floating freely, and many of them in pairs. They were nicknamed Jumbos, short for Jupiter Mass Binary Objects. Two giant planets, each the size of Jupiter, orbiting each other in the darkness with no star anywhere near. How do pairs of planets form alone in space? Nobody knows. It broke every theory astronomers had.
