You might be wondering, could one of these dark worlds ever come near us? The odds of a collision are essentially zero, space is unimaginably vast. But a close flyby is possible. In fact, some astronomers think a rogue planet may have passed through the outer solar system in the distant past, disturbing the orbits of far away objects. And right now, as you watch this, countless invisible worlds are drifting silently through our stellar neighborhood.
