You would expect a sunless planet to be frozen dead, and on the surface, it is. Temperatures plunge far below anything on Earth. But deep underground, a different story unfolds. A large planet holds onto the heat of its formation for billions of years, and radioactive elements in its rocky core keep generating warmth. Under miles of ice, that heat could melt entire oceans, hidden from the cold of space, kept liquid by the planet's own inner fire.
