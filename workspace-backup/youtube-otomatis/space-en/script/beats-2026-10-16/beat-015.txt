So what happens when we finally get there? Nothing dramatic, sadly for the movies. Two hundred and fifty million light years is so far that the universe will keep expanding around us the whole way down. By the time we arrived, if we ever could, the Great Attractor would have merged and grown into something new. The journey is the story, not the destination. Galaxies will merge, stars will be born and die, and the river will keep flowing long after our sun has burned out.
