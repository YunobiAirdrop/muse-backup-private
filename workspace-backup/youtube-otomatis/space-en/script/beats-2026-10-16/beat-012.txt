But wait, it gets weirder. In two thousand eight, a NASA team studying the afterglow of the Big Bang found hints that the Great Attractor itself is moving. Whole clusters of galaxies, including ours, might be caught in an even bigger current called dark flow, streaming toward something beyond the edge of what we can ever see. If that is true, then the Great Attractor is not the final answer. It is just another leaf in an even bigger river.
