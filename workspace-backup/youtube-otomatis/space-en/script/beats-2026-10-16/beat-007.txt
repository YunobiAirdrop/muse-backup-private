Then, in twenty fourteen, the picture got even bigger. Astronomer Brent Tully mapped the motions of thousands of galaxies and found that we all belong to one gigantic structure. He named it Laniakea, Hawaiian for immense heaven. Laniakea holds about one hundred thousand galaxies, spans five hundred million light years, and at the bottom of its gravitational valley sits the Great Attractor. The name comes from a Hawaiian phrase meaning immense heaven, and it fits. We are citizens of Laniakea, whether we knew it or not.
