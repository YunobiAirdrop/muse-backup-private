Just think about this for a second. In a few thousandths of a second — less time than a camera flash — a single burst releases as much energy as our Sun produces in days. Some estimates say decades. Its brightness temperature reaches ten to the thirty-sixth Kelvin, a number so extreme that no ordinary matter should be able to glow that hot, that fast. It blows my mind that something smaller than a city can briefly outshine five hundred million suns.
