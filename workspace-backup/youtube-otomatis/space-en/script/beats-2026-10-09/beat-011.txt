The twist arrived from an impossible address. FRB twenty oh one twenty E was traced to a globular cluster in the galaxy M eighty-one — a swarm of ancient stars, billions of years old. Magnetars are born from young, massive stars that die fast. They should not exist in a retirement home of old stars. Yet there it was, screaming away. Somehow, a burst engine was running in the last place anyone expected. The mystery was not solved. It had just gotten deeper.
