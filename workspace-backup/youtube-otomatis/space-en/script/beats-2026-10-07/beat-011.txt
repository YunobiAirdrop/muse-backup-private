Theory two is darker. Maybe the galaxies are there... and we just cannot see them. Perhaps the void is full of dim, failed galaxies, clouds of gas that never ignited into stars, or galaxies made mostly of dark matter, with barely any glowing matter at all. We have found a few of these ghost galaxies drifting in the void's depths. And honestly... that might be creepier than emptiness. An invisible population, hiding in the dark.
