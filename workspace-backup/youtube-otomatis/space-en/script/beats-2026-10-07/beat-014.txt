Just think about this for a second. A galaxy inside the Boötes Void is the most isolated galaxy we know. Its night sky would be almost perfectly black. No Andromeda glowing overhead. No Magellanic Clouds. Just a few unimaginably distant smudges, and beyond them, billions of light years of nothing in every direction. If anyone is looking up from a planet in one of those galaxies... they are the loneliest observers in existence.
