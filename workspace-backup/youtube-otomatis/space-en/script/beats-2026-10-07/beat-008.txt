Now here is the part that gives me chills. Imagine our Milky Way sitting at the very center of the Boötes Void, instead of here. Look up at the night sky. What would you see? Almost... nothing. A few faint smudges, maybe. The nearest galaxy would be so far away that humanity might not have discovered other galaxies until the nineteen sixties. We would have believed, for all of history, that our galaxy was the entire universe.
