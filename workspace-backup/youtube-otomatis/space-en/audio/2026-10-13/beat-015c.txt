Where do they come from? Most likely. other star systems, violently. When planets form and migrate, their gravity flings icy leftovers out of the system entirely , the scattered Oort clouds of other stars. Each interstellar object is a piece of another world that did not survive, or another solar system mid-construction. Oumuamua, Borisov, ATLAS , three shards from three different places, three snapshots of planet-building across the galaxy. Every one of them is a postcard from a place we will never visit.
