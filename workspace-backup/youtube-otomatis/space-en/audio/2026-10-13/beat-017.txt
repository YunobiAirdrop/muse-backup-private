So, was Oumuamua a rock, or was it a ship? Here is my honest answer after everything I have read: I do not know. And that is exactly the point. The universe showed us its hand, and we could not read the cards. Whether it was a nitrogen shard from a shattered exo-Pluto, or the first artifact of another civilization to pass through our sky — it forced every astronomer on Earth to ask better questions. A messenger arrived from afar. And we were listening.
