And here is the cruel truth about Oumuamua: it is never coming back. It is already far beyond the reach of our telescopes, fading into the dark between the stars, on a one-way journey that will outlast our species. The mystery left with it. But scientists are not giving up — the European Space Agency's Comet Interceptor, planned for launch around 2029, will wait in space like a goalkeeper, ready to chase down the NEXT pristine interstellar visitor. Next time, we must be ready.
