In October 2017, something entered our solar system that did not belong here. It came from the direction of another star, moving far too fast for the Sun's gravity to ever capture it. It was red, elongated, tumbling end over end — and then, as it left, it did the impossible. It sped up... with no visible engine. No tail. No jets. Nothing pushing it. Humanity's first visitor from another star system broke every rule we had.
