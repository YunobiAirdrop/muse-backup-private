Here is the scale that keeps me up at night: scientists now estimate the galaxy is FULL of these wanderers — perhaps billions of them, drifting between the stars. We only started noticing them because our telescopes finally got sharp enough. Every year, maybe one passes through our neighborhood unnoticed... or noticed for a few frantic weeks, then gone forever. Just think about this for a second: for all of human history, strangers from other star systems have been sailing past us, and we only learned to see them a decade ago.
