On July 1, 2025, the ATLAS survey telescope in Chile picked up a faint object moving on a path that did not belong to the solar system. Within days, it was confirmed: 3I/ATLAS, only the third interstellar object ever detected. Unlike Oumuamua, this one was unmistakably a comet — active, glowing, venting. And because we spotted it months before its closest approach to the Sun, every major telescope on Earth — and beyond — turned to watch. What they found rewrote the record books.
