It was found on October 19, 2017, by astronomer Robert Weryk, studying images from the Pan-STARRS telescope in Hawaii. He spotted a faint dot moving on a hyperbolic path — not orbiting the Sun at all. It had already swung past our star and was leaving, faster than it arrived. A one-way visitor. No comet or asteroid from our own system moves like that. That night, the sky gave us something we had waited centuries to find: our first visitor from another star.
