Half a million books. One night of fire. And a mystery we're still trying to solve.

I stood on the corniche of Alexandria watching the same harbor Alexander's ships once used — and I couldn't stop thinking about what vanished here. In this episode of Lost Empires, I trace the library from Alexander's wild dream to Caesar's torch, from Eratosthenes measuring the Earth with a stick to Cleopatra's last lamplit evenings — and the uncomfortable truth that the fire wasn't the whole story.
