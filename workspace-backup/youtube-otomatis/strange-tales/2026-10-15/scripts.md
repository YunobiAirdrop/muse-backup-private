- Hook A: "Millions of people hear a sound that does not officially exist."
- Hook B: "A low hum that never stops — and only SOME people can hear it."

## Short 2 — lluvia-de-peces (hook terkuat: A)
- Hook A: "In this town, fish fall from the SKY."
- Hook B: "Every year, the rain in Honduras brings fish. Nobody knows how."
- Hook C: "Thousands of fish, still alive, falling with the rain."
- Naskah (90 kata): In Yoro, Honduras, it rains fish. Every year during the rainy season, storms roll over a small field — and tiny silver fish fall from the sky. Thousands of them. Witnesses have watched this for over a century. A local festival celebrates the miracle every year. Scientists think waterspouts may lift fish from underground rivers, then drop them miles away. But no one has ever seen it happen in the sky. The Lluvia de Peces — fish rain — remains a mystery. If this blew your mind, hit subscribe for more strange tales.

## Short 3 — blob-slime (hook terkuat: A)
- Hook A: "This creature has no brain — yet it SOLVED a maze."
- Hook B: "One cell. No brain. Smarter than your GPS."
- Hook C: "Scientists call it The Blob — and it can learn."

Yet scientists placed it in a maze with food at the exit, and it found the SHORTEST path. Not randomly — it solved it, and remembered the solution. It even learned to ignore bitter tastes, and passed that memory to other blobs. One cell, zero brain, and it can navigate, decide, and learn. Science cannot fully explain how.
