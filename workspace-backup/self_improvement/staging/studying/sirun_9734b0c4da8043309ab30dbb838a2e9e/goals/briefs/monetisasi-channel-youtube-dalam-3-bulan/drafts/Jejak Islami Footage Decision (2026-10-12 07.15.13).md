# Jejak Islami Footage Decision

Tonight's Jejak Islami video needs a decision from you before it goes out at 19:00. The long video, "Nabi Musa AS - Laut Merah Terbelah," was built yesterday with footage from Mixkit under a restricted, non-commercial license, because Pexels was blocked by a CAPTCHA when the production run tried to get its usual clips.

I think that video should be held, or rebuilt with clean footage, rather than published as it stands. It is meant to sit on a channel working toward monetization, and footage that is not licensed for commercial use puts exactly that goal at risk. One video is not worth trading against the channel's application later. My lean is to hold tonight's slot and swap the restricted clips for properly licensed ones, even if that means Jejak Islami misses a day.

The second thing I found is quieter but just as real. Comments have switched off again on the newest videos, even after the full audit on October 10 left every channel clean. In last night's check, four newest videos had comments disabled: @ceritarakyat862, @EchoesOfThePast-e9m, @CosmosUnknownTV on the Bootes Void video uploaded that same afternoon, and @TheDarkMindFiles-s6i. Morning checks hit the same wall on other channels, including @StrangeTales-g6n.

I read that as an upload pipeline problem, not a settings problem on any one video. Fixing these four by hand would repeat what the October 10 audit already did, and it did not hold for even a day. So I will treat the fix as a pipeline one: comments verified on for every video right after upload, as part of the upload itself, instead of another one-off cleanup.

For tonight, I only need your call on the Jejak Islami video: hold it while the footage is replaced, or let it run knowing the license risk. Everything else here I can move on without you.
