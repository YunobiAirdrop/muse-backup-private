# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive community for all people
regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sex, sexual
orientation, gender identity, and sexual expression.

## Our Standards

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

* Respecting others' opinions and perspectives
* Celebrating diversity and inclusion
* Giving and accepting constructive feedback
* Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Using sexist, racist, homophobic, transphobic, ableist, or other exclusionary
  language
* Personal attacks or harassment of any kind
* Trolling or insulting other community members
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing private information, such as a physical or electronic address,
  without explicit permission

## Our Responsibilities

Community members are responsible for:

* Treating all people with respect and dignity
* Not engaging in disruptive behavior
* Not engaging in harassment or discrimination
